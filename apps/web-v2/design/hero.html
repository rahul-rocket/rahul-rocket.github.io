Elevation 1 — card, gradient hairline, soft shadow

A card is elevation 1: surface, hairline, and a gradient border that brightens on hover. It is deliberately not glass. Glass is elevation 2 and is reserved for the header, the command palette, and the mobile sheet — small, fixed-position surfaces where backdrop-filter costs nothing. On a card grid it costs a re-sample per card per frame, and it is also what makes a portfolio look like a template.
